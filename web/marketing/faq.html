Frequently asked questions

Common questions about prayer times, accuracy, and the app.

Where do prayer times come from?

Miqaat uses the Aladhan API, a widely used service that calculates prayer times based on your location and chosen method.

Why do times differ from my local mosque?

Mosques may use different calculation methods or Asr juristic schools. Open Settings in the app to choose the method that matches your community — common options include ISNA, MWL, and Umm Al-Qura.

Can I add times to Google Calendar?

Yes. After fetching your times, click Download .ics calendar and import the file into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, or any app that supports .ics files.

Does Miqaat store my location?

No. There are no user accounts. Your location is sent only to calculate times for your current request — it is not saved on our servers.

Does "My location" work on mobile?

Yes, when the site is served over HTTPS. Your browser will ask for permission before sharing your GPS coordinates.

Is Miqaat free?

Yes. The web app is free to use with no sign-up required.

Will there be a mobile app?

Yes — native apps for the App Store and Google Play are planned, with prayer reminders and offline-friendly features.
