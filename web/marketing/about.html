About Miqaat

What is Miqaat?

Miqaat (ميقات) means a meeting point — a stopping place on a journey. Miqaat is a calm, minimal tool to find prayer times wherever life takes you: at home, at work, or in a new city.

Accuracy

Prayer times are calculated, not measured. Different communities use different methods. Miqaat lets you choose your calculation method and Asr school in Settings so times align as closely as possible with your local practice.

Always verify with your local mosque if precision is critical for religious obligations.

Data source

Prayer time data is provided by the Aladhan.com API.
