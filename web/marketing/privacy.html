Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Miqaat ("we", "the app") is a prayer times tool. We collect minimal data and do not require user accounts.

Information you provide

  • City and country — when you search by location, these are sent to our server to fetch prayer times.
  • GPS coordinates — if you use "My location", your browser shares coordinates with our server. We do not store this data after your request completes.

What we do not collect

  • No user accounts or passwords
  • No persistent storage of your location on our servers
  • No sale of personal data to third parties

Third-party services

Prayer times are calculated via the Aladhan API. When you request times, your location (city or coordinates) is forwarded to Aladhan to perform the calculation. See Aladhan.com for their policies.

Cookies and analytics

Miqaat does not use tracking cookies at this time. If privacy-friendly analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated.
